# Vibe Vault

A personal digital vault to keep your music, hobbies, memories, and little pieces of life in one beautiful place.

## How to choose

* Open Vibe Vault.
* Explore the different sections.
* Add or view your favorite music, hobbies, memories, and life moments.

## Features

* Personal music section
* Hobbies section
* Memories section
* Life section
* Clean and aesthetic design
* Dark and light mode
* Responsive layout
